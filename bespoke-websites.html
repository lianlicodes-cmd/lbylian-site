Your work is already strong. It just isn’t always seen that way online.

I structure, edit and direct how your work is presented, so it can be understood quickly and remembered.

Turnaround 3 to 5 weeks, from start to launch.

Structure

What is seen first, what is held back, and how people move through the work. Structure determines whether the work is understood or overlooked.

Visual direction

How the work is presented visually — through typography, spacing and images, so it carries the right weight and presence.

Bespoke development

Built from scratch in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No templates, no page builders.

Responsive layout

Designed for desktop, tablet and mobile. Layout and spacing are adjusted for every screen size.

How I work

A focused, collaborative process built around your content and how it needs to be seen. The aim is always the same: make the work clear quickly, and hold attention.

01

Project brief

We start with your content: your images, your text and the projects you want to show. This helps define what the site needs to do and how it should be structured.

Having a clear selection of work and a basic visual direction makes the process smoother and more precise from the start.

02

Site structure

I organise your pages, content and navigation so everything is easy to follow and each element finds its right place.

03

Visual design

Typography, spacing and images are worked through to support your work, without ever competing with it.

04

Image preparation

Your images are carefully prepared for the web so they stay sharp, load properly and present the work at the right quality. This includes resizing, optimisation and structured file naming to support both performance and image SEO.

05

Development and refinement

The site is built and tested to work smoothly across all screens, with close attention to performance and accessibility.

06

Launch and handover

Before going live, we check together that everything works as expected. Once the site is live, updates and changes can be handled by me and booked in as needed.
